Oops Story for Kids! Baby Duck Says Sorry

Oops! Baby Duck runs too fast and bumps Baby Pig's apple basket. Duck hides, says it was not me, and tries to fix it alone, but it only gets worse. Then Duck says sorry, and the two friends pick up the apples together. A gentle story about making mistakes, saying sorry and helping to fix things, for toddlers and preschoolers.
