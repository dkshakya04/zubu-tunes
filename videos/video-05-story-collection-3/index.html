Short Stories for Toddlers 🧤🦆🐻 Sorry, Brave & Waiting Stories

Four brand new funny stories in one video for toddlers and preschoolers. Baby Fox loses her mitten in the snow, Baby Duck says sorry after an oops, Baby Mouse and Baby Frog meet a wobbly shadow, and Baby Bunny learns to wait for slow Baby Bear.
