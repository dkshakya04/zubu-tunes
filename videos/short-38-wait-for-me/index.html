Wait For Me Story for Kids! Baby Bear and Baby Bunny

Baby Bunny loves to race, and slow Baby Bear can never keep up. Bunny hops ahead three times, until a wobbly log makes her stuck and Bear comes to help. A gentle story about patience, waiting for friends and walking together, for toddlers and preschoolers.
