Wobbly Shadow Story for Kids! Baby Mouse and Baby Frog

Baby Mouse and Baby Frog see a big wobbly shadow on the path! They tiptoe, say hello, and take a brave hop, then peek behind it together. It is only a silly balloon! A gentle, giggly story about being curious, looking together and looking before you worry, for toddlers and preschoolers.
