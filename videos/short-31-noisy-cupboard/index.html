The Noisy Cupboard! 🐻🐱 Bear and Cat Story for Kids

Bump, bump! Bear and Cat hear a noise in the cupboard. Is it a lion? Together they look... and find a tiny kitten! A gentle, giggly story about being brave together, for toddlers and preschoolers.

Bear and Cat were playing before bed.

Then... bump, bump! A noise from the cupboard!

Bear! What was that?

I do not know. Let's listen.

Maybe it is a big lion?

Lions do not fit in cupboards!

Mew... mew... A tiny sound. Somebody small was calling.

That does not sound big. Let's look together.

Bear and Cat opened the door, slowly, slowly...

It was a tiny kitten!

Aww! Hello, little kitten!

Do not worry, little one. We will help you find your mama.

Together, we are brave!

The end!
