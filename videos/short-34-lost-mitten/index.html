Lost Mitten Story for Kids! 🧤🦊🦉 Baby Fox and Baby Owl

Baby Fox loses her red mitten in the snow! Baby Owl helps her search three times: under the snow pile, up the hill, and back along the path. A gentle winter story about not giving up, thinking calmly, and sharing a warm mitten with a cold friend, for toddlers and preschoolers.
