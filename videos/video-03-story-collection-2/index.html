The Noisy Cupboard

Bear and Cat were playing before bed.

Then... bump, bump! A noise from the cupboard!

Bear! What was that?

I do not know. Let's listen.

Maybe it is a big lion?

Lions do not fit in cupboards!

Mew... mew... A tiny sound. Somebody small was calling.

That does not sound big. Let's look together.

Bear and Cat opened the door, slowly, slowly...

It was a tiny kitten!

Aww! Hello, little kitten!

Do not worry, little one. We will help you find your mama.

Together, we are brave!

The end!

Zubu and the Lost Star

One night, Zubu was going to sleep.

Then a tiny voice came from the window... Help! Help!

Who's there?

I fell from the sky! I lost my way home!

Don't worry, little star. I will help you!

Zubu and the star set off. Up, up and up!

On the way they met a cloud. The cloud said...

The sky is up there! Keep going, friends!

Then they reached the moon.

Hello, little star! Your family is waiting for you!

And look! The star's whole family!

Thank you, Zubu! You are my best friend!

Bye bye, little star! Shine bright!

Zubu came back home and slept happily.

Helping a friend is the best thing of all!

Goodnight, friends!
